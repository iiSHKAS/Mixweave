Mixweave (previously released as Sonux) is a modified version of
[NC1107/Sink](https://github.com/NC1107/sink), starting from upstream commit
`b30fe5c787b2512bf935d1d5230e8d6057a76a3b` (Sink release `v0.1.26`).

Sink and Mixweave are licensed under GPL-3.0-only. The upstream license and
copyright history are retained. Mixweave includes substantial interface and
audio-engine modifications made in 2026, including configurable channels,
playback processing, test controls, and 7.1 spatial rendering.

Mixweave is an independent project and is not an official Sink release.
